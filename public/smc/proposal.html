/ The prototype

An agentic recovery layer that catches drop-offs before they happen — and credits the right partner when they don't.

Three connected screens: a real-time onboarding companion that intervenes at the exact friction point, an AI-assisted KYC risk scorer for the ops team, and a live partner attribution dashboard that compresses the lead-to-commission cycle from weeks to days.

/ How it gets built

A 90-day plan, scoped to ship the first measurable win in 30.

Days 0-30 · Diagnose

  • Instrument the existing eKYC funnel end-to-end (Mixpanel/Amplitude) — most broking platforms don't have stage-level drop-off in one place
  • Pull last 90 days of failed onboardings, cluster failure reasons
  • Shadow 5 sub-brokers and 5 ops agents to map the real workflow vs the documented one
  • Ship: a single funnel dashboard + the top 3 friction hypotheses, prioritised by recoverable revenue

Days 30-60 · Ship the Companion

  • Stage 1 of the AI Companion live on the top friction point (likely Aadhaar OTP / Video KYC)
  • Branch / video-KYC fallback flow integrated with existing SMC branch network — turning a moat into a feature
  • A/B test against the current flow; target 8-12% lift in stage-2 → stage-3 conversion

Days 60-90 · Scale + Partner

  • Roll Companion to all stages; ship Ops KYC scorer to deflect ~40% of manual review queue
  • Partner cockpit MVP for top 200 sub-brokers — live lead status, AI nudges, near-real-time commission visibility
  • Compress payout cycle from 30-45 days towards 7-day rolling
